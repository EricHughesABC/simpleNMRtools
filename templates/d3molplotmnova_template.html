Interactive Controls

C
Toggle node labels between experimental and calculated (predicted) shifts. Carbons with no HSQC-assigned node appear as grey placeholders while calculated shifts are shown.
Rotate
R / L rotate 10° clockwise / counter-clockwise; Shift+R / Shift+L rotate 90°. Also: Shift + scroll, or a two-finger twist on a trackpad.
Pan
Click and drag on empty space in the diagram to pan the view.
Zoom (shrink/expand)
Scroll, or pinch on a trackpad/touchscreen, to zoom the molecule in and out.
Reset view
Double-click anywhere on the diagram to reset pan, zoom, and rotation.
Reassign a peak
Drag a carbon node onto another node to move or swap its shift assignment.
Peak details
Hover over a node to see its full assignment details.
